## The entry

> You promised the diet starts Monday. It is 3am on a Tuesday. I have the receipts, and the cheesecake.

A smart fridge texts its owner at 3am: it kept the receipts, and it kept the cheesecake too. One line, 101 characters — under the 140-character cap.

The joke lands on the fridge's voice, not a human excuse: a small machine that has been awake all night, keeping score, and choosing 3am to say so. The diet promise, the wrong day of the week, and the evidence in the crisper drawer do the work in one breath.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`, exactly as written — no trailing newline added. Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
